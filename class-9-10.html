Building Strong Foundations for Future Success

A focused secondary school learning journey that strengthens subject knowledge, analytical thinking, communication, problem-solving and examination readiness.

Secondary Education at Maria’s Podar Learn School

Classes 9th and 10th are important stages of secondary education. Students begin to work with more advanced concepts and develop stronger academic discipline and independent study habits.

The learning journey focuses on understanding concepts, applying knowledge, practising regularly and developing the confidence required to approach academic challenges.

Students are encouraged to develop analytical thinking, effective communication, problem-solving abilities and responsible learning habits while preparing for the next stage of education.

Key Focus Areas

  • Strong Subject Foundation
  • Conceptual Understanding
  • Analytical Thinking
  • Regular Practice
  • Examination Readiness
  • Independent Learning
Academic Learning

Core Subject Areas

Students strengthen their understanding of important academic areas while developing the ability to analyse, apply and communicate what they learn.

Mathematics

Logical reasoning, mathematical concepts, calculations, problem-solving and application of mathematical knowledge.

Science

Understanding scientific concepts through observation, reasoning, application and systematic study.

Languages

Reading comprehension, writing, vocabulary, grammar, communication and confident expression.

Social Science

Study of society, history, geography, civics and important concepts related to our world.

Computer & Digital Learning

Digital awareness, computer skills and responsible use of technology for learning.

Academic Development

Study planning, revision, practice, self-learning and preparation for higher levels of education.

Class 9th to 10th FAQ

Which classes are included in this programme?
