About Our Maria's Podar Learn School Nilanga

Maria's Podar Learn School Nilanga

Quality Education, Wherever We Go
CBSE Affiliation No.: 1131276
School Code: 31263
Classes Offered: Nursery to Grade X

Maria’s Podar Learn School, Nilanga is a CBSE-affiliated school located in Nilanga, Latur District, Maharashtra. As a school associated with the Podar educational network, we are committed to providing students with a structured, progressive and quality-oriented learning environment.

At Maria’s Podar Learn School, we believe that there can be no compromise when it comes to the quality of a child’s education. Our focus extends beyond academic achievement to the overall development of every student through discipline, communication skills, values, creativity, sports, cultural activities and character building.

Mrs. Anjum Samir Patel

(Director of Maria's Podar Learn School Nilanga)
