About Our School

Maria’s Podar Learn School, Nilanga is a CBSE-affiliated school located in Nilanga, Latur District, Maharashtra. As a school associated with the Podar educational network, we are committed to providing students with a structured, progressive and quality-oriented learning environment.

At Maria’s Podar Learn School, we believe that there can be no compromise when it comes to the quality of a child’s education. Our focus extends beyond academic achievement to the overall development of every student through discipline, communication skills, values, creativity, sports, cultural activities and character building.

Our Leadership

The school is led by Principal Mr. Vishnu Raj, an education professional from Kerala with qualifications in M.A. English Language & Literature and B.Ed. His leadership philosophy emphasizes academic discipline, effective communication, teacher development, student-centred learning and continuous improvement in educational standards.

The Principal, academic team and management work together to maintain a positive and professionally organised school environment where every child receives appropriate guidance, encouragement and opportunities to grow.

Our Teaching Team

Our strength lies in our dedicated team of qualified and committed teachers. Our educators strive to make classroom learning meaningful, interactive and accessible while giving individual attention to students according to their learning needs.

Along with academics, the school gives importance to Physical Education and Sports under experienced guidance, encouraging students to develop fitness, confidence, discipline, teamwork and sportsmanship.

Education Without Compromise

At Maria’s Podar Learn School, quality education is our priority, not an option. We continuously work towards maintaining high standards in:

  • Academic excellence and conceptual learning
  • English communication and language development
  • Discipline and character formation
  • Sports and physical education
  • Cultural and co-curricular activities
  • Student safety and well-being
  • Technology-supported learning
  • Regular assessment and academic monitoring
  • Parent-school communication
  • Holistic personality development

Our aim is not merely to prepare children for examinations but to prepare them to become confident, responsible, knowledgeable and compassionate individuals capable of succeeding in a changing world.

Our Vision

To create a learning environment where every child receives quality education, discovers their potential and develops the knowledge, confidence, discipline and values required for a successful future.

Our Commitment

No compromise on education.

No compromise on discipline.

No compromise on student development.

No compromise on quality.

At Maria’s Podar Learn School, Nilanga, we believe that every child deserves an education that creates opportunities for a lifetime.
