PRIMARY GROUP

Nursery to UKG A Joyful Beginning to Lifelong Learning

A nurturing early-learning journey designed to help young children explore, communicate, create, develop confidence and build strong foundations for their future education.

Growing Through Learning, Play & Exploration

Maria’s Podar Learn School Nilanga provides an engaging learning environment for children in the Nursery to UKG years.

These early years are an important stage in a child's development. Children begin to develop communication, early academic understanding, social skills, confidence and independence.

Our early-learning programme encourages children to explore their surroundings, express their ideas, participate in activities and develop a positive attitude towards learning.

Key Focus Areas

  • Language and communication
  • Early number concepts
  • Creativity and imagination
  • Social and emotional development
  • Physical and motor development
  • Confidence and independence
Learning Areas

Building Skills for the Early Years

Children are given opportunities to learn through age-appropriate activities, interaction, creativity and exploration.

Language & Communication

Developing listening, speaking, vocabulary, expression and early communication skills.

Early Numeracy

Introducing numbers, counting, patterns, shapes and simple mathematical concepts.

Reading Readiness

Encouraging interest in stories, books, sounds, letters and early reading experiences.

Art & Creativity

Drawing, colouring, craft and creative activities help children express their imagination.

Music & Rhymes

Rhymes, songs and musical activities encourage participation, rhythm, listening and expression.

Activity-Based Learning

Games, activities and hands-on experiences make learning engaging and meaningful.

Programme Journey

Nursery • LKG • UKG

Each stage supports children as they gradually develop greater confidence, communication and school-readiness skills.

01

Nursery

A gentle introduction to school life through communication, play, sensory exploration, simple routines, stories and enjoyable activities.

02

LKG

Children strengthen language, early number understanding, fine-motor skills, creativity and participation through activities.

03

UKG

Children develop stronger foundations in reading, writing, numeracy, communication and school readiness for the next academic stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Nursery to UKG – FAQ

Some common questions about the early-learning programme.

What classes are included in Nursery to UKG?

The programme includes Nursery, LKG and UKG as the early-years learning stages.

What does early learning focus on?

Early learning focuses on communication, early literacy and numeracy, creativity, social development, physical development and positive learning habits.

How are children encouraged to learn?

Children can learn through stories, conversations, games, art, music, activities, classroom interaction and exploration.
