Primary Learning at Maria’s Podar Learn School

Our primary years focus on building strong academic foundations while developing confidence, creativity, communication skills, curiosity and positive values in every child.

Strong Foundations

Developing essential academic knowledge and learning skills from the early years.

Child-Centred Learning

Learning experiences designed around children's needs, interests and abilities.

Learning Through Activities

Encouraging students to learn through activities, exploration and creativity.

Our Primary Learning Areas

Languages

Reading, writing, vocabulary, communication and language skills.

Mathematics

Numbers, calculations, logical thinking and practical problem solving.

Environmental Studies

Understanding our surroundings, nature, community and environment.

Science & Exploration

Encouraging observation, questioning, experimentation and discovery.

Art & Creativity

Art, drawing, creativity and activities that encourage self-expression.

Digital Awareness

Developing basic digital awareness and responsible technology use.
