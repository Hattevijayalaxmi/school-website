Class 1st to 5th Building Strong Foundations for Lifelong Learning

A structured and engaging learning journey designed to help children develop strong academic foundations, communication skills, creativity, confidence and positive learning habits.

Primary Learning at Maria’s Podar Learn School

The Class 1st to 5th years are an important stage in a child's educational journey. These years help children strengthen their basic concepts while developing curiosity, communication and independent learning habits.

At Maria’s Podar Learn School Nilanga, the primary learning programme focuses on age-appropriate academic learning along with activities that support creativity, confidence, collaboration and overall development.

Children are encouraged to ask questions, explore ideas, participate in classroom activities and gradually take greater responsibility for their learning.

Key Focus Areas

  • Strong foundation in core subjects
  • Reading, writing and communication development
  • Mathematical thinking and problem solving
  • Creativity and activity-based learning
  • Social skills and responsible behaviour
  • Confidence and independent learning habits
Learning Areas

Developing Knowledge, Skills & Curiosity

The primary years provide opportunities for children to strengthen their academic understanding while exploring different areas of learning.

Languages

Development of reading, writing, vocabulary, comprehension and effective communication.

Mathematics

Building number sense, calculation skills, logical thinking and problem-solving abilities.

Environmental Studies

Encouraging children to understand their surroundings, nature, people and everyday life.

Science & Exploration

Developing curiosity through observation, questioning, exploration and simple activities.

Art & Creativity

Opportunities for drawing, creative expression, craft and imaginative activities.

Digital Awareness

Introducing age-appropriate digital awareness and responsible use of technology.

Primary Journey

Class 1st to Class 5th

Each class builds upon previous learning and supports children as they gradually develop greater confidence, knowledge and independence.

03

05

Primary education is also about helping children become confident, curious, responsible and collaborative learners.

Communication

Critical Thinking

Creativity

Giving children opportunities to express themselves through creative activities.

Collaboration

Physical Development

Independence

Helping children develop responsibility, confidence and positive learning habits.
